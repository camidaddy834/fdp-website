## The project

FDP designed the food service for a Frisco ISD high school built to move a large
student body through breakfast and lunch quickly. The servery runs **long,
curving serving lines** — quartz-topped counters with blue mosaic fronts set
along a **daylit glass commons wall** — with multiple parallel stations, hot
wells and cold pans, grab-and-go merchandising, snacks, and point-of-sale at the
end of each line.

Behind the servery, FDP specified a deep **scratch-cooking production kitchen**:
stacked convection and combi ovens under exhaust hoods, a tilting braising pan,
dedicated **walk-in cooler and freezer**, glass-door hot-holding and reach-in
refrigeration, a full **warewashing room**, pot-and-pan wash, and generous dry
storage. The layout keeps delivery, storage, production, service, and dish
return on clear, non-crossing paths.

## The outcome

The finished facility pairs a bright, modern servery with a high-capacity
kitchen built for cooking on site — a cafeteria sized and organized for the
demands of a large Frisco ISD campus.
