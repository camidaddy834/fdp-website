## The project

Yarbrough Elementary School opened in **2015** as the first of two Midland ISD
elementary projects FDP would design a decade apart — the second being
[Lone Star Trails Elementary](/work/lone-star-trails-es/). Same district, same
architect, same kitchen equipment contractor; two distinct food service
environments.

At Yarbrough, FDP designed a kitchen and serving line for **approximately 800
students**, featuring a **full dish room, hard trays, and a custom serving line**
built for daily operations. The servery is wrapped in a bright, multi-color
mosaic tile and a warm yellow soffit, with a curved tray slide, hot wells, cold
pans, and refrigerated grab-and-go — a cheerful, hard-working space that has fed
the campus for a decade.
