## The project

FDP designed the food service for Bailey Junior High in Arlington ISD, built to
move a full campus through breakfast and lunch quickly while giving the space
real school spirit. The serving area runs **multiple parallel serving lines**
wrapped in bold **"Home of the Bailey Rams"** graphics, with overhead menu
boards, hot wells, cold pans, and dedicated **pizza and grab-and-go** stations
so students can split across lines instead of waiting in one.

Behind the servery, FDP specified a complete **scratch-cooking production
kitchen** — stacked convection and combi ovens under exhaust hoods, a tilting
braising pan, walk-in cooler and freezer, dry storage, hot-holding cabinets,
and a dedicated warewashing room — along with the point-of-sale and queuing
layout that keeps the lines flowing.

The result is a durable, high-capacity junior high cafeteria that serves fast,
cooks fresh on site, and looks like it belongs to the Rams.
