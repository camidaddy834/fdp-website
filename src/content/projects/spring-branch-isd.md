## The project

FDP designed a refreshed serving line for Spring Branch ISD, trading a dated
cafeteria counter for a sleek, retail-style setup that keeps lunch lines
moving. A **black-tile serving counter** runs the length of the space, pairing
hot service and self-serve beverages with **open-air refrigerated
merchandisers** stocked for grab-and-go — cold entrées, milk, and snacks that
students can grab without waiting on a hot line.

Overhead glass shelving and clear sightlines let students see their options at
a glance, and **point-of-sale stations** at the end of the counter speed up
checkout. The result is a clean, high-throughput line that serves a busy campus
quickly while looking more like a modern café than a traditional cafeteria.
