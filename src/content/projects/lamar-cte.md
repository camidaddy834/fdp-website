## The project

FDP designed the food service for the Lamar CISD Career & Technical
Education Center, a district facility where the Culinary Arts program is a
headline attraction. The building pairs professional-grade teaching space
with real production capacity.

At its core are **culinary teaching kitchens** — rows of mobile stainless
work tables with stools, **ceiling-mounted retractable cord reels** feeding
power to each station, overhead demonstration monitors, and full hot lines
with Wolf ranges, griddles, and ventilation hoods. A dedicated **baking and
pastry lab**, finished in a blue-and-white tile accent, is equipped with
planetary mixers, speed racks, and proofing and holding cabinets.

Out front, the program's **Bistro** gives students a branded, student-run
service venue. Behind the teaching spaces, FDP specified a full
**production kitchen and servery** — combi and convection ovens, a long
serving line with beverage and coffee stations, hot and cold wells, and
holding cabinets — so the center can both teach and feed.

The result is a facility designed to move students from a teaching station
to a working line to a real dining venue, all in one building.
