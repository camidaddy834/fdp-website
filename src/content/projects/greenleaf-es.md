## The project

FDP designed the food service for Greenleaf Elementary School in Splendora
ISD, with a servery built to move young students through lunch quickly and
safely.

The space uses **two parallel serving lines** flanked by stainless guide
rails, letting the kitchen run multiple points of service at once. The
serving fronts are finished in a bright **green vertical tile** with
stainless counters, glass sneeze guards, and heated and refrigerated wells.
Behind the lines, FDP specified **combi ovens** and the supporting
production equipment that keeps a busy elementary cafeteria running.
