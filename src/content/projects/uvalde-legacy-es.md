## The project

FDP designed the food service for Legacy Elementary School, a new campus
for Uvalde CISD. FDP's scope — the kitchen and servery — sits inside a
building designed to be a bright, hopeful place for the community's
youngest students.

The **servery** greets students with a glossy green-tile wall and
eggplant-tiled serving fronts, stainless counters, glass sneeze guards, a
tray rail, and a dedicated cashier station, backed by tall reach-in
refrigeration. Behind it, FDP specified a complete **production kitchen**:
tilting braising pans and a tilt skillet under an exhaust hood, stacked
Blodgett convection ovens and Rational combi ovens, pot and utensil racks,
prep tables, and water filtration — everything needed to cook from scratch
for the campus each day.

The kitchen and servery serve a campus built around light and color, from
its sunlit learning commons to the central courtyard — a new landmark for
the district.
