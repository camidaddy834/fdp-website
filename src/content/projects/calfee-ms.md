## The project

FDP designed the food service for Calfee Middle School, giving the new
campus a bright, branded servery.

The serving line pairs **quartz solid-surface counters** with custom
wood-grain fronts that carry the school's **"W" mark**, lit from above by
Wells heated display shelves. A central **scramble-style serving island**
lets students approach multiple stations at once instead of waiting a
single line, with guide rails wrapping the perimeter. Reach-in refrigeration
and a supporting kitchen complete the service area.
