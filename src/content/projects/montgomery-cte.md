## The project

FDP designed the food service for the Montgomery ISD Career & Technical
Education Center, where culinary students learn in a space built to
commercial standards.

The centerpiece is a large **culinary teaching lab** — mobile stainless
work tables and stools arranged around demonstration stations, with
**ceiling-mounted retractable cord reels** dropping power to each work
surface and a demonstration hot line of ranges, griddles, and ventilation
hoods along the wall. Behind it, FDP specified a **production kitchen** to
support both the instructional program and daily service.

The program also includes the **Cafe at the Den**, a student-facing coffee
and dining venue with a tiled service bar and a warm, wood-accented dining
room, plus a bright **servery** of solid-surface serving lines with heat
lamps, hot and cold wells, and glass merchandising that overlooks the
commons.

Together, the spaces give students a path from classroom instruction to
real customer service.
