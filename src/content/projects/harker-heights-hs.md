## The challenge

Harker Heights High School needed to feed a large student body quickly, and
its existing cafeteria was built around a conventional straight serving line.
Killeen ISD wanted a dining experience that offered more variety and moved
students through faster — without expanding the building's footprint.

## The design solution

FDP reorganized the space into a **food-court layout** of themed serving
venues, each branded as its own micro-restaurant so students can head
straight to what they want instead of waiting one line:

- **Einstein Burgers & Grill**, **La Cocina** Mexican fare, and
  **Michelangelo's Pizzeria** for hot entrées
- **Plato's Daily Fare**, **Garden Fresh Salads**, **Deli Express**, and
  **Ala Carte Alley** for lighter and grab-and-go options
- **H2O etc.** beverages and a dedicated coffee and bakery bar

The serving venues use **curved stainless counters** with heated display
shelves, refrigerated bases, glass sneeze guards, and LED-lit fronts.
Behind them, FDP specified a rebuilt **back-of-house production kitchen** —
stacked combi ovens under a new ventilation hood, stainless prep lines, and
a full warewashing room — to supply every station through the day.

## The outcome

The renovation replaced a single line with multiple parallel points of
service, giving students more choice and shortening wait times within the
same cafeteria space — a modern, food-court-style servery backed by a
production kitchen built to keep it running.
