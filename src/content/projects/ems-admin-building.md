## The challenge

Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD's new administration building brings district staff
together under one roof, and the district wanted dining to be a reason people
gather there — not an afterthought tucked in a back corner. The food service
had to serve a daily lunch crowd quickly while feeling more like a modern
workplace café than a cafeteria.

## The design solution

FDP designed the building's **staff café and servery** as the social anchor of
the commons. A **curved, tile-clad serving counter** wraps a feature column at
the center of the room, with a **cook-to-order hot line** and display cooking
behind it, a **grab-and-go and coffee bar**, and self-serve beverages — all
coordinated with a full back-of-house production kitchen and ventilation.

The servery opens directly onto a **daylit, two-story commons**: communal
quartz-topped bars, café tables under a warm wood-slat ceiling, and
**outdoor dining terraces** that look out over the surrounding landscape. The
layout lets staff move from line to seat without crossing paths, and keeps the
space working for meetings and events between meal periods.

## The outcome

The result is a workplace dining destination — a café that pulls the whole
administration building together around good food, daylight, and views, with a
production kitchen built to serve it every day.
