## The project

FDP renovated the serving area at Humble High School, reworking the
Wildcats' existing commons into a modern, multi-station servery without
expanding the building.

The design distributes service across **several solid-surface serving
islands** rather than one line, with illuminated perforated-panel fronts
that glow white, stainless counters, and glass sneeze guards. A dedicated
**pastry and bakery display case** anchors one island, and integrated
point-of-sale stations are built into the counters. ADA guide rails thread
the stations through the existing commons — complete with the campus's
civics column wrap — giving students more parallel points of service and
shorter lines in the same footprint.
