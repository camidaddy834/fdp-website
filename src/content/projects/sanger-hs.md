## The project

FDP designed the food service for Sanger High School to do double duty — feed
the campus and teach the next generation of cooks. At its heart is a
**CTE culinary teaching kitchen**: individual student cooking stations with
**ceiling-mounted retractable cord reels**, a demonstration range line with
commercial Vulcan ranges, griddles, hoods, and a wall of KitchenAid mixers,
plus a display screen so students can follow along with each day's lesson.

Adjacent to the lab, the **E100 Bistro** gives culinary students a real,
branded service venue to run. Behind both, FDP specified a full
**production kitchen** — convection ovens, a dedicated **warewashing room**,
three-compartment and prep sinks, walk-in cooler and freezer, and ice
production — so the daily nutrition program and the teaching program share one
well-organized back of house.

The result is a facility that serves lunch today and trains cooks for tomorrow.
