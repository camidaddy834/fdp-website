## The challenge

A resort casino needed to replace an aging buffet with a food hall that could
run around the clock, absorb unpredictable gaming-floor surges, and still deliver
signature-restaurant quality.

## The design solution

A single high-capacity central kitchen anchors seven front-facing concepts,
letting each stall run a lean line while heavy production happens out of sight.
Redundant cooking and refrigeration keep service continuous through cleaning and
maintenance cycles.

## The outcome

The hall holds full service 24 hours a day, and covers per hour rose well beyond
the buffet it replaced — with a fraction of the labor per seat.
