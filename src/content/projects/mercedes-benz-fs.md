## The project

When Mercedes-Benz Financial Services built its new 200,000-square-foot
regional office at AllianceTexas in North Fort Worth, FDP designed the employee
café at its heart — a bright, modern dining environment meant to make the
building somewhere people genuinely want to be. The campus was named **Best New
Office Project** at *D CEO*'s 2019 Commercial Real Estate Awards.
