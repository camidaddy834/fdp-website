## The project

FDP designed the food service for Thornton Elementary in Arlington ISD,
pairing a cheerful, student-friendly servery with a full scratch-cooking
production kitchen behind it.

Out front, **curved serving counters** finished in bright watercolor-dot
graphics run beneath a sculpted green-and-gold soffit, with hot wells, cold
pans, and overhead sneeze guards sized for young students. Behind the serving
line, FDP specified a complete back-of-house kitchen built for cooking from
scratch at volume — **stacked convection and combi ovens, a tilting braising
pan, walk-in cooler and freezer, a dedicated warewashing room, and ample dry
storage** — so the campus can prepare fresh meals on site every day.

The result is a cafeteria that looks inviting to kids while giving the
nutrition staff a well-organized, high-capacity kitchen to work in.
