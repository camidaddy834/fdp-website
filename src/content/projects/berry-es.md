## The project

FDP designed the food service for Berry Elementary in Arlington ISD, giving the
cafeteria a serving experience that's as fun to look at as it is efficient to
run. The **serving lines are wrapped in color-changing LED panels** that glow
through purple, pink, teal, and blue beneath bright quartz countertops — a
simple, durable way to make the space feel special for young students.

Behind the lines, FDP specified a complete **scratch-cooking production
kitchen**: stacked combi and convection ovens under exhaust hoods, a tilting
braising pan, walk-in cooler and freezer, dry storage, and a dedicated
warewashing room. Hot wells, cold pans, and self-serve sections along the line
keep students moving quickly through breakfast and lunch service.

The finished facility pairs a bright, welcoming servery with a serious kitchen
built to cook fresh meals on site at elementary-school volume.
