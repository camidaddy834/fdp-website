## The project

FDP designed the culinary arts center at El Paso Community College — a teaching
facility anchored by a wood-fired brick oven and an open servery, giving
students a professional environment to train in. The project was featured in
*Texas Architect* and showcased in FCSI's *The Americas Quarterly* Project
Showcase issue.
