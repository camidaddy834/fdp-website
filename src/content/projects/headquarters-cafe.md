## The challenge

A newly consolidated headquarters wanted a single amenity that would make the
office worth the commute. The catch: one central kitchen had to serve breakfast,
lunch, and an evening grab-and-go without ever feeling like a cafeteria.

## The design solution

We zoned the space into a grab-and-go market, an exhibition grill, and a
barista bar, each able to open and close independently as the day shifts. A
compact but flexible production kitchen sits behind all three, sharing cold and
dry storage to keep the equipment package lean.

## The outcome

Participation climbed past every projection, and the café became the building's
default meeting spot — exactly the daytime gravity the client was after.
