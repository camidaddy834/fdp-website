## The challenge

The university's original two-line servery was built for a campus half its
current size. Lunch rushes produced 20-minute queues, and the back-of-house
couldn't support the fresh, made-to-order menu students expected.

## The design solution

We replaced the linear serving lines with eight themed micro-restaurant
platforms arranged around a central seating commons. Each platform carries its
own cooking suite, so production happens in front of the guest and load spreads
across the room rather than stacking at a single line.

A consolidated commissary in the basement feeds all eight platforms through a
dedicated service corridor, keeping deliveries and waste entirely out of the
guest experience.

## The outcome

Peak throughput nearly doubled while the dining footprint stayed the same.
Average wait time fell under four minutes, and post-renovation satisfaction
scores rose sharply in the first semester.
