## The challenge

A growing medical center was moving from scheduled tray line to on-demand room
service, and the existing kitchen couldn't support cook-to-order volume or the
infection-control and life-safety standards the shift required.

## The design solution

We designed a dedicated room-service production line alongside a separate staff
café, with strict separation of clean and soiled flows, redundant refrigeration,
and finishes and ventilation specified to clinical standards throughout.

## The outcome

Patients now order meals on demand across a 24-hour window, tray accuracy rose,
and the staff café became a genuine respite for clinicians between shifts.
