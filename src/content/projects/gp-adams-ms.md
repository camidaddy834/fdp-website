## The project

FDP designed the cafeteria food service for Adams Middle School in Grand Prairie
ISD as a **food court built around the school's Panther identity**. A bold
black-panther mural in the Adams gold and navy anchors the room, and the serving
area is organized into **themed micro-restaurant venues** — Luigi's Eatery,
Ballpark Classics, Gourmet Greens, Adobe Grill, and Traditional Cuisine — each
with its own signage so students head straight to what they want.

The serving lines run **quartz-topped counters** with ribbed stainless fronts,
hot wells, cold pans, refrigerated grab-and-go and snack merchandising, and
point-of-sale stations positioned to split the crowd across multiple lines. Tall
windows wash the dining room in daylight, and FDP coordinated the back-of-house
kitchen and equipment package that supplies every venue through the lunch rush.

## The outcome

The finished cafeteria turns a single lunch period into a food-court experience —
more variety, faster lines, and a space that looks and feels like it belongs to
the Panthers.
