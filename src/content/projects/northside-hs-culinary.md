## The project

FDP designed the Culinary Arts Facility at Northside High School in Fort Worth,
Texas — a teaching kitchen built to give students hands-on experience in a
professional-grade environment. The result of a close collaboration between the
district, the builder, and our team, the facility was featured in an episode of
the *BUILT.* series.
