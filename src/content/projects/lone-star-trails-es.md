## Ten years. Two schools. One lasting partnership.

In 2015, Midland ISD opened Yarbrough Elementary School. Ten years later, Lone
Star Trails Elementary School followed.

Same district. Same architect. Same kitchen equipment contractor. Two distinct
food service environments.

At Yarbrough, FDP designed a kitchen and serving line for approximately 800
students, featuring a **full dish room, hard trays, and a custom serving line**
built for daily operations.

A decade later, Lone Star Trails introduced a fresh approach: an **angled
Mod-U-Serve serving line**, a **Corian tray slide** selected during the design
process, and **illuminated blue-tile counter fronts**.

Different designs. A decade of continued trust.

Thank you, Midland ISD, for the opportunity to contribute to both projects — and
to our partners who helped bring these spaces to life.
