## The project

FDP designed the serving area for Spring Woods High School in Spring Branch
ISD, giving the campus a modern, fast-moving point of service.

The serving line is wrapped in **glossy black tile** with white
solid-surface counters, glass sneeze guards, and integrated
**point-of-sale** stations so students can move through quickly. A tall
**open-air refrigerated merchandiser** anchors the grab-and-go offering,
with reach-in refrigeration and snack and beverage displays built into the
line. Overhead menu displays round out a servery designed to turn a busy
lunch period efficiently.
