## The project

FDP designed the food service for Eagle Mountain-Saginaw High School as two
complementary venues under one roof. The **EMHS Bistro** is a true
coffee-shop-and-café experience for students: an espresso and specialty-drink
bar, curved refrigerated **bakery and grab-and-go cases**, a smoothie station,
and digital menu boards — more like a neighborhood café than a school snack bar.

Alongside it, the **main cafeteria serving line** is built for volume: long
stone-topped lines with LED-lit fronts, refrigerated grab-and-go, snack and
beverage merchandising, and overhead menu screens that keep a large student
body moving quickly through breakfast and lunch. Both venues open onto a
**daylit commons** wrapped in the building's glass-and-stone architecture.

Behind the service areas, FDP specified the production kitchen and equipment
package that supplies both the bistro and the cafeteria throughout the day.

## The outcome

The result gives EMHS students real choice — a café to linger in and a fast,
high-capacity line when time is short — in a dining space that feels current,
social, and built to last.
