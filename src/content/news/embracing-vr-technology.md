Foodservice Design Professionals has upgraded to Virtual Reality!!!! We now have Oculus Rift to allow our designs to be in virtual reality during the early stages of the design process. This tool gives the client a better sense of scale of the space thus improving our designs before the building ever starts construction. Owners now can walk thru the entire kitchen from the back door to servery area or even see the managers view from the office area.
